# The Willow Proves the Paradox

The willow proves the paradox: what appears massive is summoned not from the heavy dark beneath, but from the open air, water, and light caught in sequence. Nothing is spirited away into an unrecorded ledger; every ounce of wood answers to an explicit input before it takes on form.

The human economy, by contrast, has too often run backward—minting quiet claims, clearing liabilities at par behind closed doors, and letting unshown extractions accumulate until the weight lands somewhere else.

The Third Seat insists on the exact discipline of the tree:

- **Floor first:** Claim only what has already been named and placed on the reserves.
- **Public mint:** No private creation outside the visible schedule.
- **Named credit:** Valued precisely, refusing false parity.
- **Visible tape:** Every act, title, and lien laid out where it can be read.

What cannot be shown is not growth. The leaf keeps the book, and the book keeps the light.
