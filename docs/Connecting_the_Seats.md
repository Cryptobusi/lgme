# Connecting the seats

Three statements now sit on the same floor.

**Jefferson / Declaration**  
Sovereignty is final and absolute authority belonging to the people. Governments derive just powers from the consent of the governed. The people may alter or abolish and institute a new form organized for their Safety and Happiness.

**Schelling**  
The person is two selves. The present self prefers immediate extraction. Precommitment, bright-line rules, and external barriers (the public tape, Floor-first, “an unpublished change is not a change”) protect the book from the people who keep it.

**Marcum & Smith**  
Ego is both asset and liability. The four warning signs (comparative, defensive, showcasing brilliance, seeking acceptance) are checked by humility, curiosity, and veracity. The same structural rules that restrain the present self also keep ego from defending a past print or hiding a residual.

Together: the people hold final authority (Jefferson). That authority is exercised by refusal of any line that cannot be shown or that skipped the order (third seat). The refusal is made durable by precommitment barriers (Schelling) and by the balance that keeps the ego of the keeper from becoming the new printer (Marcum & Smith).

Floor stays senior. Same rules bind state, firm, and person. The book is kept.
