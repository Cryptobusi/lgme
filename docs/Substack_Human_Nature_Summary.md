## The Psychological Ground Truth
Greene’s 18 laws show how evolutionary heuristics (irrationality, narcissism, envy, conformity, short-sightedness, generational myopia) override rational self-interest. Emotions decide in milliseconds; logic often only justifies. Childhood patterns, social masks, and the repressed shadow drive compulsive and self-sabotaging behavior. Collectives amplify herding and fickleness.

These map directly onto economic pathologies: hyperbolic discounting → bubbles and debt; Veblen effects → positional consumption; executive hubris → value-destroying M&A; herding → systemic risk; Minsky cycles → stability breeding fragility.

## The Relational Counterweight
Durable coordination requires faculties that classical *Homo economicus* ignores:

- **Rational Compassion** — a living floor paid from real surplus so survival is not held hostage to market volatility.
- **Personal Sovereignty** — the structural right of refusal (the Third Seat).
- **Strategic Mercy** — clean-slate relief that releases frozen labor instead of compounding traps.

Extractive models produce credit impairment, capital-weighted plutocracy, and welfare traps. Regenerative / commons models compress the Gini coefficient, raise the velocity of money, and reduce default contagion and property offenses.

## The Architectural Alternative
Egonomic Anonymous (egonomicanonymous.live) and lgme.app implement this on public rails (Hedera Consensus Service):

**Floor → Mint → Issue → Tape → Charge → Agent → Thin → Desert**

- Senior Living Floor from documented surplus before any discretionary claim.
- Public Tape: every mint, lien, conversion, and agent act is append-only and queryable.
- Third Seat: cryptographic sovereign refusal of non-compliant lines.
- Vote by Doing: governance earned by verified contribution, not capital weight.
- Anti-rehypothecation and identity-anchored credit history prevent the debit abandonment that killed historical mutual-credit systems (LETS, etc.).

It is not a new currency or a new monopoly issuer. It is the existing rails plus a visible sequence that binds state, firm, and person equally. If it cannot be shown, it is not policy. Sovereignty is the refusal.
