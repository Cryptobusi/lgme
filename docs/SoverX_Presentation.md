# SoverX — LGME for Sovereigns

**Trial presentation**  
**Status:** Temporary trial under lgme.app  
**Date:** 13 October 2026

---

## What it is

A simple agent that teaches the third seat. The person keeps the book by seeing what must be shown.

## Why it exists

Most people do not read the order first. SoverX walks them through it with plain words, a small dashboard, and a mock reward.

## How a cycle works

1. Choose an input (act, receipt, contract, promise, claim, refusal).
2. Add a short comment you can share.
3. See the dashboard: public, residual, Floor, refusal.
4. Complete the cycle → mock 1 $lgme recorded.

## What it does not do

- Does not issue real $lgme.
- Does not enroll you.
- Does not move value.
- Completions are mock only.

## Floor still senior

Even in the trial the mock reward is shown only after Floor is acknowledged. Same rules bind the agent.
