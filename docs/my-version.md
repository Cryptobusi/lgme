# My Version

A member who has installed lgme can tailor the app on their own phone and, if other members agree, offer that setup as their own named version.

This is settings, not code. A version can change the theme, the order of the three actions, a few extra words on those buttons, which category chips show, the suggestions in the AI dock, the one-line help for a field, and whether the actions or the chips come first. The names IdidIt (verified receipt), IwantIT, and IhavIT stay visible. A version cannot add HTML, JavaScript, or new CSS. The page script rules stay as they are.

## On your phone

Open Customize from Health, from Versions, or from Help develop. You see a live preview. Reset puts the default back. Nothing leaves the phone while you are only previewing.

When you choose Offer this version, you see what changed, a name, and one line about the purpose. You confirm before anything is sent. The draft you were editing stays on the phone either way.

## Proposed version

After you confirm, the version is proposed. The name is checked against versions already offered. If local AI is on, it can help spot a near match. If AI is off, the server compares the text. A near match asks you to join that version instead of making a duplicate.

Other signed-in members can attest. The author cannot attest their own version. Each account can attest once. The page shows the count only as n of 3. There is no ranking and no streak.

## The administrator, until a member DAO exists

Until a member DAO is established, the administrator has sole right to deny.

The administrator receives a copy when a version is proposed, and another copy when three members have attested. Each copy is an entry in the owner inbox, with the full settings, and a private email that includes those settings. The administrator can deny it, with an optional reason, or approve it now. If they take no action for 72 hours after the third attestation, the version is committed.

A denied version is no longer public. It returns to the author’s phone as a private copy.

## After it is committed

A committed version has its own id and shows the author’s name. It appears in the Versions gallery. Another person can install it with one tap. That applies the settings on their phone, and they can revert to what they had before.

The author can also post it on the Health board as an IhavIT offer named Custom setup offered. Only Health is open for use. Other categories stay browse-only. The version is stored on the server. It is not written on chain.

## What stays the same

Health hashes stay on the network the site is actually using. The red testnet notice stays. Health data stays on the phone. No $Trust is involved. The count of attestations is not a score.
