# Nested commitment

## Levels

| Level | What it governs | Change cost |
|-------|------------------|-------------|
| L0 | Operational rule (Floor first, named residual, public mint, titled zero) | Ordinary recorded act |
| L1 | Amendment of L0 | Delayed window, public notice, second reading |
| L2 | Amendment of L1 | Longer delay, multi-seat confirmation, time-lock that cannot be shortened in the same act |

Each level is harder to change than the one below it.

## L2 rule

Any change to the amendment path (L1) must itself be posted as a named, titled line at least one full public cycle before it can take effect, and must be confirmed by a second, independent reading that cannot be supplied by the same seat that proposes it. Until that cycle closes, the prior L1 path remains in force. An attempt to shorten or waive the cycle writes nothing.

## Design rules

- Strict inclusion: a change at level *n* may not weaken a constraint at level *n*−1 in the same act.
- Default-closed at every level: silence writes nothing.
- Constitutive tape: an amendment that does not appear on the open record is not an amendment.
- Symmetric binding: the nested path applies identically to state, firm, and person.
- No same-act exploitation: the act that benefits from a weakened barrier cannot be the act that weakens it.

## Mapping

- L0 is the existing order: Floor seniority, public mint, named charge before residual, titled remainder (including zero), “an unpublished change is not a change.”
- L1 is the slower recorded path already required for Floor changes and off-schedule windows.
- L2 is the rule above: the amendment path itself cannot be shortened or waived in the moment of temptation.

## Nested test

Can this extraction or relaxation be completed without a prior, public, categorical commitment at L0, and without completing the slower nested path at L1/L2 that binds every seat equally and leaves a checkable mark?

If yes, the nesting is incomplete.

Floor stays senior. Same rules bind all three. An unpublished change is not a change.
