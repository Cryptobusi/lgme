What this is, and what it is not

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Commentary and research, not an offer

lgme is commentary and research on money, credit, and public record-keeping. Nothing on this site is an offer to sell, or a solicitation to buy, any token, security, or other financial product. Nothing here is investment, legal, or tax advice.

No promise of return

Nothing on this site promises or implies any return, payout, or gain from holding, buying, or using any token. Sections of the whitepaper that describe treasuries, tokenization, or distributions are design research and are not implemented.

Tokens mentioned here

$TRUST (Hedera token 0.0.10607411) may be referenced as part of the project's public record. $TRUST is DOVU's utility token for metering writes on its Authority Trail protocol (see authority.dovu.ai/economy). On the Hedera public record, the token has no admin key, and its treasury and sole supply (mint) key are held by DOVU's issuance contract 0.0.10607410. This site and project do not control the token's treasury, keys or minting. Do your own research and only act within the laws that apply to you.

No exemption is claimed

The Hederica frame — a public record of civic work, hours instead of a speculative mint — is the mission. It is not a 501(c)(3), a 501(c)(4), or any other exempt organization. There is no determination letter and no fiscal sponsor. The charter lists the limits and the work still to do. An hour described here is not cash, not a deposit, and not a security.

Gifts and support

A gift sent to a personal account named on these sites is voluntary and non-refundable. It buys nothing: no tokens, no hours, no equity, no returns, and no services. It is not a charitable contribution. No fiscal sponsor is named. It is not tax-deductible.
