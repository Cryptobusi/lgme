Dear friend

The narration the film was made to speak, in one voice, as a letter. It follows the study film: the coffee, the pump, the house, the car, the card, the late loan, and cash. Each part is about fifteen seconds. In the manner of a patient letter and an unhurried teacher. Not those men. Not financial advice, and not a new coin.

0:00 The cup

Dear friend. Do not hurry the number. Live the question. A purchase does not vanish. The coffee you buy becomes the next person's wage. The circuit is as ordinary as rain.

0:15 The menu

What is strange is not the cup. It is the extra promise, written in a room you were never shown. The spreadsheet is the menu. The life is the meal. Do not confuse them.

0:30 The pump

Then the pump. The price jumps, and a whole town feels it by evening, and nobody can point to the line that moved. If you cannot see the claim, you are being told a story about a ghost.

0:45 The house

A house of four hundred thousand is a long letter from years you have not lived. Interest is a vine. The vine is not wicked. It was planted in a room with no window.

1:00 The car

The car is the same letter, shorter and louder. You drive the machine. The paper drives you. A pause is not, we are fine. A pause has a date. An IOU is not cash in the glove box.

1:15 The card and the loan

The card raises its own price while you sleep. The student loan scolds a late Tuesday as if lateness were a sin. Food, the roof, the bus. Those needs are senior. They are met first. What remains may be nothing. Nothing is allowed.

1:30 Cash

And cash, which we call the real thing, is only the oldest mask. Same rails for a friend and a stranger. No quiet lane. A tool, an afternoon, a lesson. No cover charge. Receipts, or it did not happen.

1:45 Three chairs

Set three chairs, not three kings. The public may tax and schedule the printing. The shop may issue. You do not rule them. You sit beside them, and you may say, show me that line, or I refuse it.

2:00 The pen

The helper may explain. It may not hold the pen. A clever tool that no longer answers to a person is not wisdom. It is a replacement. Do not hand it your name.

2:15 The chair

Be patient with what is still unsolved. A shared book makes a quiet print expensive, because anyone may open it. Happily ever after is not a feeling. It is a letter you can carry home. The person keeps the chair.
