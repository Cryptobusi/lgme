MEMBER COVENANT & MUTUAL CREDIT CHARTER
Circle Name: Egonomic Mutual Clearing Circle
Platform Reference: lgme.app / Sovereign Provenance Protocol

Preamble & Operating Stance
This Circle operates on the discipline of Sober Poverty: voluntary restraint, zero speculative leverage, and direct physical clearance. Our common ledger is exclusively a unit of account and a tool for reciprocal exchange. It does not carry interest, accumulate financial yield, or interact with debt-based banking systems.
By signing below, the Member enters a covenant with fellow participants to anchor every recorded credit in genuine human labor, useful material goods, and unencumbered peer accountability.
Section 1: The Twelve Articles of Participation
Article 1 (Functional Need): Credits shall be drawn solely to satisfy immediate, real-world personal or household needs. Speculative purchasing, resale arbitrage, and balance hoarding are prohibited.
Article 2 (Direct Labor): Credit is backed by productive capacity. Members agree to settle debits through direct services, physical goods, or verified manual work. Third-party brokering is barred.
Article 3 (Open Audit): Account balances and clearing histories are fully visible to all peers within the Circle. Members agree to transparent, continuous ledger verification.
Article 4 (Hard Credit Limits): Negative balances are hard-capped at an initial threshold of -50 credit units (maximum -200 upon community review). When the limit is reached, outbound debits suspend until counter-services restore the balance.
Article 5 (Zero Yield & Non-Speculation): Circle credits will never bear interest, dividends, or appreciation. Credit is a medium of clearance, not an asset for financial accumulation.
Article 6 (Lean Tooling): Transactions record directly via open, minimal protocols with zero dependence on proprietary intermediaries or closed banking infrastructure.
Article 7 (No Commercial Noise): Internal communication channels are reserved for concrete listings and verifiable offerings. Commercial advertising, promotional spam, and hype are grounds for revocation.
Article 8 (Bilateral Resolution): Delivery disputes or service discrepancies must be addressed directly and immediately between the two transacting parties before petitioning community mediation.
Article 9 (Immediate Gift Clearance): Voluntary aid, teaching, and neighborly assistance recorded as gifts clear instantly. They create no balancing debt, social lien, or future claim against the recipient.
Article 10 (Geographic Proximity): Core exchanges must remain locally verifiable within the physical territory of the Circle to ensure personal familiarity, inspection, and direct delivery.
Article 11 (Velocity Over Hoarding): Surplus positive balances must be spent back into fellow members’ offerings within a 90-day window rather than held idle as claims over the community.
Article 12 (Zero External Debt): The Circle remains completely unencumbered by outside bank debt, fiat collateral, or external liens. Member claims exist strictly within peer ledgers.
