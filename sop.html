1. Diagnosing the failure modes of legacy economic SOPs

Between the initialization of human stewardship and final historical settlement, economic life unfolds within an unfinished, contested middle [1]. Modern commerce and civic administration currently fail to govern this historical middle due to three structural defects.

Escaping Chronos

The speculative trap

Exhausted by the grinding friction, inflation, and entropy of quantitative time (Chronos), legacy markets flee into speculative bubbles — attempting to manufacture instant windfalls (Kairos) through unbacked issuance and debt illusions rather than anchoring value in verifiable work [2].

Uncounted human capital

The verification blind spot

Vast reserves of real human capital — mentorship, neighborhood care, informal labor, and micro-enterprise — never reach a balance sheet because traditional banking and auditing rails cost more to clear than the underlying act is worth [3], [4].

Asymmetric rules

Opaque extraction

Conventional institutions permit the State and the Firm to issue unbacked claims, obscure yield caps, and impose retroactive fees, leaving the individual Person without institutional leverage or verifiable auditability [4].

2. The core thesis: governing the historical middle

Constitutional invariant: egonomicanonymous.live establishes three-seat symmetry — binding the State, the Firm, and the Person under identical rules — paired with Floor Seniority, ensuring basic living and operational costs are funded first from named reserves rather than unbacked claims. lgme.app is proposed as the software that would convert everyday stewardship into a zero-bureaucracy ledger of Providence through Provenance.

3. The canonical execution order

To serve as a default operating standard for economic coordination, the proposal enforces a strict, non-negotiable order across every transaction, attestation, and automated workflow.

  1. 1Floor
  2. 2Mint
  3. 3Issue
  4. 4Tape
  5. 5Charge
  6. 6Registrar
  7. 7Thin
  8. 8Residual

Floor is funded first. Mint follows the same schedule for everyone. Issue names the bill against attested effort. Tape writes the receipt before any charge. Charge is one price, with no retroactive cut. The registrar can explain the line and cannot tap pay. Thin is the honest spread, not a hidden fee. Residual is what remains, and it can be nothing.

The four-pillar architecture

1 · Egonomics guardrail

Floor seniority and pre-commitment

Cryptographic, default-closed execution rules. Subsistence and baseline operational floors are funded first from existing reserves, before any surplus, fee, or secondary distribution is calculated.

2 · Hedera Consensus

Constitutive public tape

Low-cost immutable consensus timestamps (IDidIt receipts) [4]. Captures micro-contributions, civic deeds, and enterprise milestones at near-zero cost, eliminating the overhead that previously erased informal human capital.

3 · DOVU OS model

Verifiable workflow provenance

Tamper-evident process capture (Define → Execute → Issue → Verify) [3]. Replaces slow, manual third-party audits with automated, defensible evidence attached directly to labor, supply chains, and ecological stewardship.

4 · Ownera FinP2P

Zero-integration liquidity

Application-layer orchestration across chains and legacy rails [5]. Allows verified receipts and cooperative hours to clear peer-to-peer, or route across institutional networks, without bilateral middlemen or rent-seeking gateways.

4. The fourfold mandate: can, should, must, and will

Can

Technical and economic viability

High-throughput consensus networks (Hedera), verifiable audit infrastructure (DOVU OS), and universal FinP2P orchestration routers (Ownera) are already operational in production environments [3], [4], [5]. By relying on lightweight consensus attestations (IDidIt receipts) rather than heavy speculative instruments, the proposal scales toward micro-transactions without fee volatility or administrative bloat.

Should

Ethical and civic restoration

Human vocation within the passage of time is not to decode speculative timelines, but to practice faithful stewardship and build durable structures [1], [2]. The proposal realigns digital infrastructure with true oikonomia (stewardship of the shared household) — rewarding verifiable effort, safeguarding human dignity through Floor Seniority, and enforcing the rule: if it cannot be shown, it is not policy.

Must

Systemic necessity

Legacy administrative bureaucracies are buckling under compliance friction while unbacked monetary expansion erodes purchasing power [4]. Without a constitutive, tamper-evident public tape that binds State, Firm, and Person under symmetric rules, economic coordination fractures into either authoritarian ledger control or destructive financial speculation.

Will

Market and evolutionary pressure

Capital, human labor, and autonomous agents migrate toward the lowest-friction, highest-trust settlement rail [4]. A standard that eliminates bilateral reconciliation disputes, blocks hidden extraction via default-closed checks, and records previously uncounted informal and cooperative value outcompetes legacy intermediaries on cost, settlement speed, and institutional legitimacy.

5. Phased implementation roadmap

Phase I · Civic and cooperative proof-of-work

Inscribe the day’s work

Scope: non-banked community labor, peer education, open-source stewardship. Deliverable: standardize the inscription of daily contributions onto the public tape as non-speculative IDidIt receipts, backed by verifiable workflow evidence [3].

Phase II · Enterprise and agentic guardrails

Bind the agent to the order

Scope: micro-enterprises, supply chains, autonomous execution agents. Deliverable: bind automated agents and commercial invoices to the canonical order — Floor Seniority and provenance verification required prior to execution [4].

Phase III · Interoperable global clearing

Route what the tape already shows

Scope: institutional liquidity pools, cross-chain and legacy settlement. Deliverable: connect local cooperative ledgers and verified enterprise receipts to FinP2P routing networks for peer-to-peer settlement across markets [5].
