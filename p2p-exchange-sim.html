lgme · SIMULATION

P2P exchange

Two people propose a line and both sign it. A third person may refuse it. A cycle of work clears only when every party has countersigned, and only when nobody’s floor is taken. Each signed copy lives in that peer’s own book.

Floor stays senior

Food, rent, and the bus are a protected balance. A settlement that would lower a peer through that floor is not settled. The tape records Blocked: Floor stays senior. A cycle is judged on the net, so equal work can clear without spending the floor.

Third seat

The third seat is the person who can read the line and refuse it. The refusal is recorded. It moves no balance and carries no penalty. The person who refuses is bound by the same order. It does not unwind a line already shown, and it does not freeze anyone else’s settled book.

Tape

Each book is append-only and hash-linked with SHA-256. Every line is signed with a key generated in this browser for that peer. Export the tape and verify it. If a copy is edited, verification fails.

IdidIt

A work receipt names the job and who did it. It is not cash. It moves a balance only when a countersigned exchange claims it, and that claim is written on each party’s own tape.
