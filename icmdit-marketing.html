IcmdIt — for donors

The best will and testament for the most benevolent desires. Your inheritance shores the Floor, funds the deeds the community has shown it needs and cherishes, and keeps the highest works of human imagination and noblest inventions intact—under sovereignty, with minimum overhead, and with assurance that the wishes cannot be quietly rewritten.

Positioning

Your capital can become the will that serves society at its highest: Floor first for the living, residual for attested deeds and preserved inventive works, all under rules that living third seats can read and refuse. Sovereignty is the refusal. The tape keeps the book after you are gone.

Core messages

  • Best will, intact. Benevolent desires, highest imagination, and noblest inventions remain titled and executable. No private rewrite.
  • Floor first. Living costs and designated reserves are paid before residual. Capital does not become free cash.
  • Shown deeds. Releases prefer the work the community has already made visible and cherished.
  • Minimum overhead. Low-cost public inscriptions. The agent does not mint or hold.
  • Tenacious assurance. Conditions on the immutable tape. Unpublished change is not a change. Third-seat refusal stays on the record.
  • Sovereignty. Same rules bind state, firm, and person. The person keeps the third seat.

Who it is for

Individuals and families who want a bequest that shores the Floor and funds shown civic and inventive work. Advisors seeking a programmable complement to paper trusts where clients prioritize transparency and Floor protection. Holders of dormant assets who prefer attested, community-visible outcomes.

Channels

  • Plain pages on this desk (this note, the formulation, Study, Hederica).
  • Short explainers that walk one example: commit → Floor paid first → residual against shown deeds or published rules → refusal recorded if order is skipped.
  • Outreach to complementary-currency, mutual-aid, and estate-planning communities that already discuss programmable bequests and Floor-style circuits.
  • Advisor one-pagers mapping conditions onto familiar trust concepts while noting the public tape and third-seat check.
  • Public tape summaries of Floor status and attested deeds so prospective donors can read the book first.

Assurance framing

IcmdIt is elective and subject to the published order. It creates no new mint and no cash claim. Open items (enrollment, oracles, legal recognition of on-tape conditions) remain open. The donor’s leverage is precommitment and visibility, not control by private discretion after death.

Call to action

Read the book before you commit. Review the formulation, walk the order, and see the Floor and tape as they stand.
