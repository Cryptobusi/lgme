Third Seat

The Third Seat is your seat, held by you alone, with equal standing beside the state and the firm. You may decline any exchange at no cost, no unpublished rule can bind you, and any hold on your exchange must give a reason and can be challenged, decided within 7 days or it lapses. Holds the law requires are cited, recorded, and kept no broader or longer than the law demands. The veto is recorded. It moves no balance and carries no penalty.

Tape · public ledger
