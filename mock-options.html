Summary of added parameters

The base 24 runs vary participation, service value, refusal, and mild seasonality. The options below add parameters that appear in a real deployment:

  • Attrition — people leave over the 6 months (moves, illness, disengagement).
  • Floor pressure — share of recorded value that must clear the floor before residual.
  • Service concentration — how much activity sits in a few services vs spread evenly.
  • Dispute / oracle rate — share of exchanges that need an extra check before they stay on the tape.
  • Sybil friction — cost of enrollment that reduces effective participation.
  • Time-to-clear — days an act waits before it is taped (affects usable residual).

None of these expand the coin supply. Coins stay conserved. They change how much recorded value is usable after floor, charge, and friction.
