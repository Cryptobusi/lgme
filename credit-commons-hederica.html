Executive Summary

Alternative economic coordination faces a structural dilemma: remain confined within isolated local mutual aid silos, or adopt speculative token rails that introduce financial extraction and custodial risk. The lgme.app platform resolves this dilemma by unifying the federated transport topology of the Credit Commons Protocol (CCP) with the constitutional evidentiary foundation of the Hederica Charter. Inter-community trade flows across federated clearing paths, while internal balance tracking stays a record of work. Not written to Hedera.

3. End-to-End Interoperability Sequence

  1. Relay Ingestion: An external mutual credit circle initiates a trade clearance request using standard OpenAPI 3.0 endpoints (POST /cc/transaction/relay).
  2. Constitutional Filter: The lgme.app adapter checks sender and counterparty states, confirming the action conforms to local mutual covenants and does not violate the Unconditional Survival Floor.
  3. Two-Phase Commit: Balances are provisionally locked across ledger branches during the prepare phase, preventing double-spend or dangling state splits.
  4. Consensus Deed Inscription: Upon final validation (PATCH /cc/transaction/:uuid/completed), the settlement is formatted as a standardized JSON deed package and committed to public consensus rails.
  5. Evidentiary Packaging: The transaction produces an exportable FRE 902(13/14) certificate containing hash verification headers, cryptographic signatures, and consensus timestamps.
