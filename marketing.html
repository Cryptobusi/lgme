Marketing & Adoption

This page is a plan for how a neighbor can start. It says what is already on lgme.app, and what is still only a plan. Not financial advice. Not an offer.

What gets in the way

Partly live

The words are hard

Floor, Tape, and Third Seat read like a textbook. People stall before they see a use. The glossary now puts a plain name beside each of those five terms. Not every screen uses the plain name yet.

Planned

The value stays abstract

A finished deed should become something a person can use this week: a ride, a repair, an hour of tutoring. Today the book can show the deed. It does not yet turn that deed into a shop credit.

Partly live

The public story does not match the desk

Outside, the story still sounds like a coin sale or a social platform. On the desk, you keep your own wallet and your own book. The Beta notes say that. A public campaign that says it in one line is not running yet.

Four moves

Live

1. Two layers of language

Keep the book words, and put the everyday name next to them. The five-term table is already on the site: Tape as a public ledger, Third Seat as the arbiter who can veto, Quiet Print as hidden issuance, Floor as a basic-needs reserve, and Issue as a peer promise.

Live

2. Very local time and deed banks

The health club community is the primary pilot: members, trainers, and the front desk as the place a credit can be redeemed. The campus kit remains available for tutoring, cleanup, and gear share. A deed logs with a handle and the code you already use. No coin. The first open of this site still plays a three-second coin, once.

Partly live

3. Spend a verified receipt nearby

Inside the campus pilot, a verified deed is an IdidIt (verified receipt). One of those is one campus credit, spent once on a listed offer. A shop offset in quiet hours, and a city check of volunteer hours, are still a plan. A public timestamp on Hedera, about $0.0001 a deed, is a planned option. It is not written to Hedera now.

Planned

4. Audit Your Reality

A campaign, not a product claim. Many platforms keep 8–15% of a job. A log that can be checked can cost less than a cent once a public timestamp is added. Today the log stays on the phone and the Hedera write is off, so that comparison is a plan, not a live price.

Three phases

PhaseWorkStatus on lgme.app
1. Plain language Refresh the screens so the everyday names sit next to the book words. Live. The glossary and the everyday names are on the desk.
2. A closed pilot The health club community, with the campus pilot still available. Ready. The health club page, the campus kit, and the deed log are on the site. The room still fills itself.
3. Shops and co-ops A bridge to a merchant or a co-op, using bilateral clearing. Partly live. Net out and the 7-day clearing window are on the exchange. The shop bridge is not open.

Phase 3 builds on what the desk already does: when two people owe each other, Net out proposes the smaller amount, and open IOUs sit in a 7-day window. A shop joining that loop is still a plan.
