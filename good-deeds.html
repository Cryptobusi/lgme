A deed is a piece of work with its own credit unit. The US$ value is locked when you commit, from the $lgme price at that moment. Later price moves do not change that deed.

How a category fills

Tutoring, repair, cleanup, and gear each keep a pool under the main floor. The main floor is the basic-needs reserve. The pool does not borrow from it.

On a priced deed the buyer pays the price. The provider receives 99%. The other 1% goes into that category’s pool. A donation to the category goes in whole.

How a discount is paid

A provider can post an IselIT sale with a discount, or pro bono. The pool covers the gap, up to half of what is in the pool, and up to the daily cap for that person. The cover is paid only after both people attest an IdidIt (verified receipt). If the pool is empty, the discount pauses. The buyer pays the full locked price.

A tutoring class

A tutor posts a class at $20. The learner pays $20. The tutor receives $19.80. Twenty cents stay in the tutoring pool. Neighbors can also donate into that pool.

When the same tutor offers the next class for $10, the pool can pay the $10 gap after both people attest. The loyalty rebate on an $lgme payment stays on the main floor. It is a separate line.

Posts and a free item

Each category has four posts: IwantIT is a request, IhavIT is an offer, IselIT is a priced sale, and soBeIT is a donation. Categories with more requests and finished deeds rank first and take an unassigned donation before the others.

A price of zero becomes a donation request for the deed’s locked unit. When someone funds it, half goes to the category pool and half to the main floor.

Not written to Hedera. The book is the tape on the phone, including the skim, the donation, the payout, and the split. The Third Seat is the arbiter who can veto before anything is paid.
