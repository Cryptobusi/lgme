Wisdom & motivation

The 12 principles of Sober Poverty translate directly into concrete daily operational protocols. The objective is continuous friction absorption and instantaneous self-clearing across the physical organism, the working mind, and interpersonal exchange.

Source framework: egonomicanonymous.live.

Rewards

A workout mints nothing. A reward comes from another member’s support, as a contribution or a barter, or from redeeming a club credit at the front desk. The gift clears its own ledger: zero-tracking peer support stays optional and unrecorded. There is no leaderboard, streak, or ranking.

On a published wisdom entry, support can be deed credit, $lgme, or US$. Ten percent goes to the Floor, the basic-needs reserve. Ninety percent goes to the author. The split is shown before you confirm, and the tape records it. A gift that would spend your own floor is refused. Submitting or concurring mints no credit.

Cold exposure, fasting, and heavy loading are personal choices. Check with a doctor if you have a health condition. Not medical advice.

Sober Poverty: 12 maxims

Morning Induction

Cold water wakes the marrow

Daily practice. End morning hygiene with 90–120 seconds of unheated water. No transitional warming.

Verification & clearance. Nervous system fully alert without exogenous stimulants.

Morning Induction

Silence exposes the signal

Daily practice. Zero audio, feeds, notifications, or external media for the first 60 minutes after waking.

Verification & clearance. Mental baseline established in silence before external inputs enter.

Morning Induction

Direct responsibility kills anxiety

Daily practice. Identify the single highest-friction administrative or technical task and begin it within 15 minutes of work onset.

Verification & clearance. Dread converted into concrete material progress.

Focused Work Block

Necessity concentrates the mind

Daily practice. Work in isolated 90-minute blocks with only essential terminal windows/tools active; close background monitors.

Verification & clearance. Task objective completed without cognitive residue or task-switching tax.

Focused Work Block

Fewer tools sharpen the craftsman

Daily practice. Restrict workflow to core utilities (plain text/markdown, command-line interfaces, foundational libraries) before reaching for heavy abstractions or plugins.

Verification & clearance. Code, prose, or architecture is lean, functional, and devoid of bloat.

Focused Work Block

Frugality creates the true reserve

Daily practice. Commit zero capital or commitments to speculative tools, subscriptions, or speculative dependencies.

Verification & clearance. Operational surface area remains completely self-contained.

Midday Reset & Metabolic Discipline

Fasting purifies the palate

Daily practice. Maintain water/electrolyte-only intake through the morning; defer the initial caloric window until core mental output is finalized.

Verification & clearance. Sensory receptors reset; mental acuity maintained through stable blood glucose.

Midday Reset & Metabolic Discipline

Hunger seasons the bread

Daily practice. Break fast with single-ingredient, unprocessed whole foods prepared by hand. No refined flavor enhancers or artificial sweeteners.

Verification & clearance. Satiety achieved through earned biological demand rather than hedonic stimulation.

Midday Reset & Metabolic Discipline

Walking measures the ground

Daily practice. Conduct transitions and errands on foot. Minimum 30-minute purposeful stride under open sky.

Verification & clearance. Spatial and temporal orientation anchored to natural human locomotion.

Mutual Exchange & Social Ledger

The gift clears its own ledger

Daily practice. Execute one high-leverage peer support action (technical review, direct aid, knowledge transfer) with zero tracking, expectation of return, or public signaling.

Verification & clearance. Obligation ledger remains at zero; transaction clears in the moment of execution.

Mutual Exchange & Social Ledger

Simplicity preserves sovereignty

Daily practice. Audit incoming agreements, communication channels, and accounts; decline non-essential commitments and close dormant dependencies.

Verification & clearance. Zero external leverage, recurring liabilities, or administrative entrapments.

Evening Grounding & Recovery

Labor anchors the sleep

Daily practice. Undergo 45–60 minutes of direct physical loading (compound lifts, bodyweight resistance, manual work) until genuine physical fatigue is achieved.

Verification & clearance. Physical exhaustion matches mental output; sleep initiates without artificial sedatives.
