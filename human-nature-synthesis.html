Part 1: The Laws of Human Nature

Summary of the 18 laws (via animated synthesis of Greene). Critical assessment notes strengths in biographical historiography and weaknesses in simplified evolutionary psychology.

Mastering the Self

  • Law 1 (Irrationality): Emotions drive decisions in milliseconds; rationality requires a pause.
  • Law 2 (Narcissism): Spectrum from deep narcissism to healthy self-worth channeled into curiosity.
  • Law 3 (Role-Playing): Social masks; observe micro-expressions for true intent.
  • Law 4 (Compulsive Behavior): Childhood patterns repeat under stress or power.
  • Law 5 (Covetousness): Desire is socially mediated (elusive, forbidden, rival-validated).
  • Law 6 (Short-Sightedness): Tactical reactions blind long-term consequences.
  • Law 7 (Defensiveness): Validate core identity before persuasion.
  • Law 8 (Self-Sabotage): Mindsets as perceptual filters create self-fulfilling loops.
  • Law 9 (Repression): Denying the Jungian shadow leads to projections.

Navigating the Social Collective & Long-Term Fulfillment

Laws 10–18 cover envy, grandiosity, gender rigidity, aimlessness, conformity, fickleness, aggression, generational myopia, and death denial. Durable leadership requires emotional steadiness and conscious acknowledgment of mortality.
