4. The case for Egonomic preparation

4.1 Refusal of the grant

Traditional governance relies on an external grant of authority, credit, or moral permission. Operational sovereignty begins when an agent refuses the unearned grant and stays inside an execution sequence that cannot be quietly rearranged:

Floor → Mint → Issue → Tape → Charge → Agent

4.2 The third seat

Instead of a fabricated observer, the protocol enforces auditability by three rules.

Floor priority. Essential living and operating costs are settled from verified value before ambition or speculative profit can be allocated.

No quiet print. A deficit cannot be hidden or monetized through unbacked issuance. It is recorded as an explicit refusal.

Public tape. Acceptance requires prior validation of work on a ledger that can be opened.

4.3 Immunity to the story

An engineered religion fails when the agent’s capacity exceeds the logic of the narrative. This protocol is not outbid by a better story, because it runs on structural rules with no metaphysical premise. Zero overhead. No assumption about a watcher.
