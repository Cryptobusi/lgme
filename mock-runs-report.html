More realistic options · 24 seeded runs (8 per case). Exemplary near-median runs are highlighted. Full set stored and linked below. Desk · Summary

What was run

Three cases, eight runs each. Variation: participation 62–94%, service value ±18%, refusal 2–8%, mild monthly seasonality. Coins issued relative to prefilled assets and conserved. Floor stays senior. Every exchange taped. An unpublished change is not a change.

Commentary

Conservation holds. In every run the coins issued at the start equal the coins held at the end. Value is created by the acts that are recorded, not by expanding the unit.

The gain is stable. Across all 24 runs the productivity gain sits between roughly 28% and 36%. The median is near 32%. That is the difference between acts that would have vanished under ordinary cost-of-clearing and acts that are now on the tape.

Scale does not break the pattern. The same rules produce the same shape of result at 300, 1,000, and 5,000. Participation and service mix move the absolute numbers; they do not invert the gain.

Real-world ramifications

What is being counted. Tutoring, neighbor help, snow clearing, study groups, light care. These are real acts. Under ordinary rails the cost of recording them exceeds their face value, so they never reach a balance sheet. The protocol records them for the cost of a consensus timestamp.

What the 32% means. It is not a claim of new money. It is the portion of existing work that becomes visible and therefore usable for coordination, credit, and residual after the floor. A school, a nursing home, or a neighborhood that already does this work would see it appear on the tape instead of disappearing.

What does not change. Floor stays senior. Charge is named and separate. The third seat can refuse a line that skipped the order. No cooperative is formed by these runs. No membership is offered. No token is for sale. The numbers are mock. The structure is the structure.

What would be required next. Real names replaced by real handles, real assets replaced by real prefilled or earned positions, and a live tape. The mock shows the shape. The live system would have to keep the same invariants.
