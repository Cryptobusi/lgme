$lgme loyalty discount

When a deal is paid in $lgme, the desk suggests a loyalty discount. The usual suggestion is 5%. The people in the deal can change the percent, up to the cap in the payment settings. Cash and Zelle get no discount.

Both parties sign the percent. Either one can decline, and then there is no discount. The payer still pays the full agreed price in $lgme. After that payment is final, the floor pays the discount back to the payer as a rebate.

The floor funds this rebate because the app supports the floor. This page holds no floor key. The floor operator signs the rebate in their own wallet. If the floor balance would fall through the reserve, the rebate waits. There is no penalty. The cap is US$ 25 each 30 days. The reserve is US$ 100.

Pegged to US$ at time of transaction. Values are set in US$ and converted at the live 3-second average. Pegged to US$. The price used never moves more than 5% from recent reference prices, for stable value.

Option C. Zelle

Zelle details: ask the recipient in chat / provided on request

Fiat payments (cash, Zelle) may incur additional statutory fees. Details are reviewed per transaction.

Zelle and cash are not reversible-protected. This app does not process them. A Zelle payment is not counted until the payee attests the confirmation number, and any screenshot is stored only as a SHA-256 fingerprint. Nothing is uploaded.

  1. Ask the recipient for the Zelle name in chat if it is not shown above.
  2. In your bank app, send the gift by Zelle.
  3. Save the confirmation number.
  4. On the exchange desk, choose Pay with Zelle, enter the amount, the date, and the confirmation number.
  5. Optional: add a screenshot. The desk keeps the fingerprint, not the file.
  6. The payee taps Attest, or Refuse with no penalty. Only then is it counted.
