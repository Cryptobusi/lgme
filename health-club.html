Health club community

This is the primary pilot. Members and trainers log deeds and exercise sessions. The club front desk is where a credit can be redeemed. The club keeps its own subfloor under the main floor. A deed is not $lgme, and it is not written to Hedera.

Who is here

Members train. Trainers can verify a session. The front desk is the merchant: a place that can take a club credit for a listed offer. One verified deed is one club credit, spent once.

Exercise

Start, then Finish. GPS, a photo, and motion are optional. The raw record stays on the phone. The receipt says how long it lasted, which sensors were used, and whether the location matched the club fence. Someone else in the club co-signs. You cannot verify yourself.
