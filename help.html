lgme · Help

Help

lgme.app is a tamper-evident notebook for work you do with someone else. Two people confirm a deed. The record stays on their phones. The desk does not hold keys or seeds, and it does not send a transaction.

How to start

  1. Open the exchange.
  2. Set a passphrase and a 6-digit code on this device. That unlocks the book.
  3. Log a health-club deed, or an exercise session, with a handle. No coin is required for the deed.
  4. The other person attests. Then it is an IdidIt (verified receipt).

Google sign-in: later.

FAQ

Is a deed $lgme? No. A deed credit is a mutual-credit IOU on the local tape. Paying in $lgme is a separate step and needs your own wallet.

Who can veto? The named third seat, the arbiter on that deal. A veto before payment moves nothing.

Where is the book? On this device. Export a backup if you want to carry it.

Exercise sessions

Tap Start, then Finish. GPS, a camera photo, and motion sensors are each optional. The raw points, photos, and traces stay on the phone. The receipt carries a hash, the duration, which sensors were used, and whether the location matched the club fence: yes or no. Coordinates are not published. Another person, a trainer or a member of the same private club, co-signs. You cannot verify your own session. A workout mints no credit. Support shows the terms first, the recorder can tap No, and a gift cannot spend the giver’s floor. Health and fitness keeps a subfloor: 1% of a paid contribution, plus donations. Today’s line on Start and Finish comes from Wisdom. An optional intent on the receipt is a maxim title only.

Wisdom

Sober Poverty maxims and a daily checklist live on Wisdom. The checklist stays on this device. Members can contribute an excerpt. Concurrence: at least 2 other members agree before wisdom is published. A $lgme or US$ gift splits 5% to the Floor and 5% to the App development fund. A deed-credit gift keeps the 1% Health and fitness skim. The author cannot concur on their own submission. Submitting or concurring mints no credit. Submissions stay on this device until shared listings are switched on.

The gym beta is a phone app at /gym. The Demo Health Club is labeled example and stays off your real book. Chat text stays on this phone until shared chat is connected. Chat text is not written to Hedera. My progress stays on this phone until a category is shared. The shared board starts when sync is on. Progress is not written to Hedera. Read the consumer health data notice and the breach plan. Health data stays on the phone. No ads or analytics code reads a workout.

Status

Live

The primary pilot is a health club community: members, trainers, and the club front desk as the place a credit can be redeemed. The campus pilot remains available under Other pilots. The exchange, the tape, daily Merkle roots, category subfloors, and the plain pages are on the site.

Exchange invoices and receipts use the label IdidIt (verified receipt). /ididit still redirects to the exchange. Needs 3 attestations (over $100). At or below $100, the two parties still confirm the deed. Above that, three other members sign with their device keys before anything clears. The receipt shows the pending count. Suggested third attester: group administrator, otherwise the trainer, otherwise the group creator. Only hashes go to the Hedera testnet topic.

A passkey on the exchange uses this device. The browser stores only the credential id. It does not hold a key or a seed, and it sends nothing. The passphrase still locks the book. If the browser has no passkey, use the passphrase and the 6-digit code. Google sign-in: later.

Email sign-in is coming. A .edu address is the campus option. A club domain or invite can mark a health-club member, and neither is required. KYC is email only. Full identity checks are not started.

A $lgme quote asks the public GeckoTerminal pool list, then DexScreener. No API key. The lock lasts 15 seconds and stays within 5% of the prior lock, the 24-hour price, the 7-day price, and the 30-day price. The tightest band applies. A day move past 5% holds that cap for 7 days. If history is missing, only the 15-second rule applies. The tape stores the real price, the capped price, and which cap. There is no hand-typed price. If no quote settles, the desk offers pay-in-terms or barter. DexScreener currently has no pair for this mint, so GeckoTerminal is the source that answers.

Not started

Testnet hashes are written on a schedule. The browser queues a hash. The daily job sends it. Health data is not written. Topic 0.0.489104 is refused. No mainnet write.
