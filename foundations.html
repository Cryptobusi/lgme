Abstract

Fiat and debt-money mix two operations that do not belong on one ledger. Human work adds in a straight line. Interest compounds. The second outruns the first, and the unit of account bends.

The accounting engine across lgme.app and egonomicanonymous.live, under the Sovereign Provenance Protocol and the Third Seat, is built to avoid that mix. Three results are claimed. Zero-sum mutual credit conserves attested provenance above a pre-market survival baseline. Federated clearing keeps a cross-ratio, so a local unit cannot be stretched onto a neighbor. The Two-Generation Sunset folds aged surplus back to the Floor.

1. The Floor comes before the numbers

A formal account needs a boundary. On one side is what a market may clear. On the other is what participation itself requires. In the foundations of arithmetic, a pre-numeric ground precedes counting. Here that ground is the Floor.

Ω = F₀ ⊕ E, and the edge of E does not meet F₀.

F₀ is essential nutrition, shelter, and foundational care. E is enterprise clearing. On the book now in force, maintenance of lgme.app is also part of the Floor, including after a DAO.

Axiom. The clearing operator stays inside E. A negative balance in E cannot place a lien on the Floor, and it cannot liquidate it. A recorded exchange is voluntary. It is not a sale of someone’s survival.

2. Zero-sum credit

Credit here is not an interest-bearing balance. It is one attested delivery, signed, and ordered in time. A person’s balance is attested inflows minus attested outflows.

Σ bᵢ(t) = 0

Every edge is counted once as a plus and once as a minus, so the whole ledger is zero. On a closed cycle, netting the smallest weight around the loop reduces exposure and leaves every net balance unchanged. The imbalance cancels. It does not harden into debt.

3. The ratio that a change of unit cannot bend

A single global ruler distorts a town. A town that inflates its own unit can also rob the next town, if settlement uses the raw number. Projective geometry keeps a four-point cross-ratio still when lengths are stretched.

I(e₁, e₂; e₃, e₄) = (w₃ − w₁)(w₄ − w₂) / ((w₃ − w₂)(w₄ − w₁))

Under any non-degenerate change of local unit, that ratio is unchanged. Federated clearing, including with Komunitin and Credit Commons, settles on the proportion of attested work. A node cannot export debasement to its neighbor.

4. The Two-Generation Sunset

A zero-sum ledger can still freeze if one balance runs to positive infinity and another to negative infinity. The modular inversion that sends an extreme magnitude back inside a bounded region is the model. The Sunset is that bound on enterprise balances.

The generational horizon is nominally two times twenty-five years, fifty years. Inside the active window, an unspent surplus is left whole, so present work is not punished. After that window it attenuates smoothly, by a squared cosine, and at the horizon it is zero. The same attenuation applies to a non-predatory legacy deficit, so the ledger remains zero-sum at every step. Aged surplus folds back into the Floor.

|bᵢ(t)| stays finite, bounded by maximum delivery, times degree, times the horizon.

5. No compound-interest pole

The sequence of attested exchanges can be written as a generating function. Because inflows and outflows are opposites, the sum of balances remains zero as an algebraic identity. Closed loops are the factors of a graph zeta function, and each such loop can be cleared without changing nets.

Interest-bearing debt has a generating series with a pole inside the unit disk. The Sunset kernel is a polynomial. A polynomial has no such pole. Aged surplus is the residual that returns to the Floor, and the active balance does not diverge.

Section 5, in plain terms

Section 5 is the proof that lgme.app behaves like a self-cleaning, self-balancing neighborhood scoreboard, not a snowballing debt machine. Think of the community ledger as a shared notebook. Every line is an honest promise or a verified favor.

The master timeline. A generating function folds the whole history into one continuous stream, like rolling a long strip of film onto a single reel, instead of flipping through millions of receipts. Because every line records an equal plus for the giver and a minus for the receiver, the community balance adds to zero. Nobody prints extra credit out of thin air.

Loops cancel. You fix a neighbor’s car. That neighbor tutors a baker’s child. The baker brings you bread. Three favors happened, and nobody owes anyone at the end of the week. The graph zeta formula is the loop-finder. It spots the closed circle and wipes that IOU slate, shrinking the clutter without changing anyone’s real standing.

Savings do not become a dynasty. Compound interest is a snowball. It has an explosion point, where debt and wealth run toward infinity until the system breaks. The q-series sunset puts a gentle, predictable timer on unspent surplus. You keep and use what you earn through your working life. Over two generations, leftover surplus dissolves back into the shared Human Floor: basic food, shelter, and care. On this book, maintenance of lgme.app is part of that Floor. Removing the explosion point is the proof that the ledger can run without freezing into extreme wealth or crushing inherited debt.
