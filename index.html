IdidIt receipt

An IdidIt receipt is a single Hedera Consensus Service message that records the job (what was done), the person who did it (the named issuer), the timestamp and sequence on the public tape. That message is the credit. It is not cash, not a deposit, and not a token that spends at the register. It is a named, attested record of work that sits on the tape.

As a record it is a durable claim of provenance. It can be held, shown, refused, or left unused. In that sense it is a store of attested work—nothing more. As a source of exchange value it becomes one only when it is claimed against another shown item (another receipt, a basket unit already titled, a listed price, etc.). The claim itself must appear on the tape. Until that matching claim is written and accepted, the receipt has no general purchasing power. An unpublished or unmatched receipt writes nothing that spends. Floor stays senior. If it cannot be shown, it is not policy.
