Programmable credit, used like programmable money

The mock run applies credit the way firms and states apply programmable money. The credit supports the floor. Their money stays theirs. lgme records the credit. It does not issue their money. This page is not on the live desk.

Desk

The credit

A credit here is a named line. It is not cash, not a coin, and not a balance the desk holds. The 28 October share, read from the 2027 money film, describes the dials on the firm’s and the state’s money. This run copies the use, not the issuer.

Their dials stay on their side: memory, a clock, a purpose, a grid, a pause, a machine at checkout. Those dials are not settings of lgme.

Used like their money

  1. A shown purpose. Their money can be told where it may be spent. A credit on this mock can carry a purpose the same way. Both seats can read it. If it cannot be shown, it is not on the credit.
  2. A shown clock. Their money can expire. A credit can name a time the same way. When the time is past, the line says so. The book does not hide the end.
  3. It ends at the exchange. Their purpose-bound money is supposed to become ordinary money once it settles. This credit drops its purpose and its clock when the exchange is written. It does not keep a file of how it was used.
  4. The floor first. What the credit clears supports the floor before any other claim. Their unit, if one is brought, stays their money. The credit is not that unit.

What the mock run does

  1. Name the credit. Who owes, who is owed, the purpose, and the time. All four are on the line.
  2. Floor first. The credit supports the floor before any later claim. Floor stays senior.
  3. Exchange. Firms and states may bring their own money to the exchange. That money is theirs. The credit is the book’s line beside it.
  4. Then the tags come off. When both seats have signed the exchange, the purpose and the clock leave the credit. The written line stays.

Not their dial

The mock does not put their pause, their identity rail, or their agent inside lgme. It does not mint. An agent of theirs is their clerk. Refusal stays a line on the book.
