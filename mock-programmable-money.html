Programmable money is the firm’s and the state’s

It supports the floor. It is prepared only for the exchange, as the money firms and states already use. It is not a balance, a coin, or a dial inside lgme. This page is not on the live desk.

Desk

Where it sits

lgme keeps the book. The firm and the state keep the money. The share of 28 October, from Jack Chapple’s plan to reset money in 2027, describes their dials. It does not move those dials onto this desk.

The share asked what lgme.app should change. This page refuses that. Programmable money stays with the offices that already issue it.

Their dials

These are claims in the share. They are not a new tape, and they are not lgme’s settings.

  1. Memory. A coin does not remember who held it. The firm’s and the state’s software money can.
  2. A clock. Their money can expire.
  3. A purpose. Their money can be told where it may be spent.
  4. A grid. Their terminal stops when the power stops.
  5. A pause. Their office can freeze a balance. Cash cannot.
  6. A machine. Their rails are already being handed to an agent at checkout. That agent is their clerk, not a seat on this book.

Only at the exchange

The prepared use is one. Firms and states may bring their money to the exchange. That money supports the floor. It does not enter the book as a second mint, and it does not become a balance lgme holds.

  1. Their money. The unit at the exchange is the money the firm or the state already issued. lgme does not mint it and does not hold the keys.
  2. The floor first. What they pay in supports the floor before any other claim. Floor stays senior.
  3. Then the book. The exchange is written. The dials stay on their side of the line. A condition that cannot be shown is not policy.
  4. The person still refuses. Refusal is a line on the book. It is not a new money inside lgme.

Prepared. Not built. Not inside lgme.

Nothing here is on the live desk. No clearance record, no offline rail, and no agent checkout is added to lgme. Those would put their dials inside the book.

When this is used, it is used only as exchange: money of the firms and the states, in support of the floor.

Floor stays senior. Same rules bind state, firm, and person. If it cannot be shown, it is not policy. Mock run. Not an offer.
