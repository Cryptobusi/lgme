Tuned to the thesis

The order on egonomicanonymous.live is Floor, Mint, Issue, Tape, Charge, Agent, Thin, Desert. They printed the money. The book keeps the record. The credit is fine-tuned to that order.

  1. Floor. Living costs are paid first. The credit supports the floor before any later claim. The money for that is surplus already named.
  2. Mint. The credit is not a mint. No new unit enters here. If a firm or a state brings money, that unit was already issued on their schedule.
  3. Issue. The credit is the issue: who owes, who is owed, the purpose, and the time. A named obligation. Not cash at par.
  4. Tape. Those four are written. An unpublished purpose is not a purpose. An unpublished change is not a change.
  5. Charge. A purpose is not a fee. The charge stays its own shown price. No quiet print.
  6. Agent. A clerk may read the line. The clerk does not mint, does not set the floor, and does not turn their pause.
  7. Thin. The condition is only as tight as what is shown. A wide line stays wide.
  8. Desert. After the floor, what remains is named, even when it is nothing.

The third seat can refuse a line that skipped this order. The refusal is written. Programmable credit may carry a purpose and a clock, the way their money does, and only inside this order.
