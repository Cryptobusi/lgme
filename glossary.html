lgme · Glossary

Glossary

Framework term, everyday name, everyday function. The first five lines match the home page.

Framework termFront-end human primitivePractical everyday function
Tape / Constitutive Record Public Ledger / Verified Receipt An immutable proof of completed work everyone can inspect.
Third Seat Nonprofit arbiter The $lgme self-regulating nonprofit arbiter, a check on the State and Floor seats. In a deal the Third Seat is the arbiter who can veto.
Quiet Print Hidden Issuance / Shadow Dilution Creating money or promises off-schedule behind closed doors.
Floor Basic Needs Reserve Food, shelter, and essentials secured prior to speculative spending.
Issue / Named Bill Peer Promise / Mutual Credit A non-cash IOU directly tied to human effort, not speculative debt.
IdidIt (verified receipt) Both-party receipt The record after both people confirm the deed.
IwantIT Request A member asks for a deed.
IhavIT Offer A member offers a deed.
IselIT Priced sale A sale at a locked price. A discount can come from the subfloor.
soBeIT Donation A gift. A zero price becomes a donation request.
Deed credit unit Locked value One deed’s US$ value, fixed when it is committed. It is not $lgme.
Exercise session IdidIt / verified receipt A Start and Finish record. Phone evidence stays on the device. The public line is the day, the category, the duration, the sensors used, whether location matched the club, and the support terms. A workout alone mints no credit. An optional intent is a maxim title.
Sober Poverty Daily maxims Twelve maxims and a device-only checklist for exercise and work. The source framework is egonomicanonymous.live. A maxim title can sit on a receipt. The practice stays off the public record.
Concurrence Two other members At least 2 other members agree before wisdom is published. A $lgme or US$ gift splits 5% to the Floor and 5% to the App development fund. The author cannot concur on their own submission. One concurrence per member.
App development fund Commons share 5% of a $lgme or US$ contribution. The other 5% goes to the Floor. 90% goes to the author. The split is one setting, shown before you confirm, and written on the tape.
My progress This week’s numbers Three to five categories on the member page and the exercise listing. Each shows this week, the change from last week, and a small chart. Sharing is off until the member turns it on. There is no ranking.
Health meter Self-reported A 1 to 10 score for energy, mood, and sleep. It is self-reported, not medical. It stays private unless that category is shared, and then only the number, the unit, and the week go out.
Subfloor Category pool A reserve under the main floor for one kind of work. It does not borrow from the floor.
