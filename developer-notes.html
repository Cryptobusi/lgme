Join, on the floor, is the other chair. It does not write a code. Recommend writes the slip, FLR- and four characters. Your own slip does not count. A used slip cannot be used again. When it counts, both chairs become 2× from the next step. Coins already written stay.

What holds

Launch

Pumpup The Floor is a practice life, not a mint. You press the book to launch. You name a person and a place. The Observer is the witness, and that gate is already passed. The rules are read before the floor runs.

Coins

Eight steps. A step that counts pays $lgme. A step that does not, pays nothing. The base is 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8. An invitation that another chair enters makes the next steps 2×. $lgme is a proficiency mark. It is not cash, and it is not proof.

Gates

The gates refuse a short name, an unnamed act, a blank beneficiary, a time of zero, a floor above the thinner side, a pump that does not rise, and a reading that does not match the line. Your own slip is not a join.

Hints

Steps 1 to 3 show a hint. After a step counts, the running list keeps what was entered. Each step changes the button color. The example receipt for EgonomicAnon is labeled practice, not an attestation.

Where it falls short

Ids

The protocol line is contributor, beneficiary, work scope, time spent, and the witnesses, plus when and where. The floor has the scope, the time, a name, and the word Observer. It does not have ids. A witness who cannot refuse is not a verification.

This browser

The book stays in this browser. Another device does not see the chair, the slip, or the line. A record that cannot be shown is not policy.

Pictures

A picture is a reduced copy stored with the book, three at most. A folder is a list of names and sizes. The files themselves are not kept.

First screen

The example receipt sits above the launch book. Hints stop at step 3. A live receipt is a long random id. The example uses receipt-egon-0001.

Build next

Show the line

Let one closed line leave the browser. A plain copy of the five fields, the place, the time it was counted, and a short receipt.

Stable ids

Give the contributor and the beneficiary a stable id, not only a name.

Witness

Leave the Observer as the practice witness. A later witness has to be able to refuse.

Picture bytes

Stop keeping picture bytes in the book. Keep that a picture was shown, and its name.

Book first

Put the launch book above the example. The example is a sample, not the door.

Later hints

Add one hint to steps 4 through 8, the same length as the first three.

Short receipt

Make a live receipt short enough to read aloud, in the manner of receipt-egon-0001.

No chain yet

Do not attach a public chain, a wallet, or an attestation standard yet. The practice would start to look like proof it is not.
