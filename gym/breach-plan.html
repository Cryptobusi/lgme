Breach response plan

Draft pending counsel review. Not legal advice.

Consumer health data · Gym

Workout and health records live on the member’s phone. This plan covers a case where that data leaves a phone, or a credit hash is exposed, without the member’s permission.

  1. Detect. A member report, a support note to desk@lgme.app, or a sign that health data left a device without permission starts the clock.
  2. Contain. Stop the path that leaked it. Turn sharing off for the affected member. Keep new health data on the phone. Do not put the leaked record on Hedera.
  3. Assess. Write down what left, whose records, and whether it was health data or only a credit hash. Health data and a credit hash are not the same notice.
  4. Notify. Tell each affected member within 60 days. This follows the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule. If 500 or more people are affected, also notify the FTC and the media.
  5. Log the lesson. Record what happened, who was told, and what changes so the same path is closed. Keep that log with the operator, not in the member’s workout book.

Contact for a suspected breach: desk@lgme.app.
