Gym · Breach plan · Help

On this phone

For adults 18 and older. Collecting workout info on this phone and sharing progress are separate choices.

Workout logs, the health meter, GPS points, photos, your legal name, your signature, and the salts that lock this book stay in this browser. lgme does not keep a copy on a server. Your name and track record stay here too, unless you separately choose “Send my name and track record to my sponsors.”

Shared only with permission

Sharing is a separate choice. It stays off until you check “Share selected progress with people I choose.” You pick a category, you see a preview, and you can turn it off later in Settings. People you did not choose do not get the numbers.

Hedera

Health data never goes on Hedera. A sponsor report can show credit hashes. Nothing on this page is anchored or written to Hedera. A workout stays on this phone.

Ads and analytics

No ads code and no analytics code reads your workout. Gym pages load scripts from lgme only.

Delete everything on this phone

One tap removes the local health book, private covenant fields, and helper key. It also discards local salts. This cannot be undone.

Not medical advice

The health meter is self-reported. It is not a diagnosis, a treatment, or a training plan. Stop when you want to.
